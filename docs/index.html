A look inside

Every role gets its own dashboard, built around what they do all day.

Built for every role in the hospital

Role-based dashboards for patients, doctors, ten hospital staff roles and admins, all in one app.

Patients

  • Health notes: a personal journal; share a note with a doctor and get comments back.
  • Messaging with read receipts, voice notes and PDF attachments.
  • Video consultations with full incoming-call screens.
  • Doctor AI: structured answers to medical questions.

Doctors

  • Clinical tasks routed to one of 15 departments or to named staff.
  • Live tracking: get notified the moment a task is completed.
  • Shared notes: review what patients share and reply in context.

Hospital staff

  • Task inbox for your department and personal assignments.
  • One-tap completion that notifies the requesting doctor.
  • AI MRI analysis for lab staff, with a downloadable PDF report.

Admins

  • User directory with active and deactivated accounts.
  • On-call management for staff.

AI where it helps

Two Python services sit alongside the app.

Brain MRI analysis

  • MobileNetV3-Large with a convolutional attention layer, fine-tuned on four classes: glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumour, no tumour.
  • Grad-CAM heatmaps show where the model looked.
  • Generates a PDF report with probabilities, images and risk factors.
Upload scan→MRI check→Classify→Grad-CAM→PDF report

Medical chatbot

  • Instruction-tuned LLM (Mistral 7B) with automatic fallback models.
  • Answers are reshaped into consistent sections: overview, key facts, when to see a doctor, treatment.
  • Every answer carries a medical disclaimer.
Question→LLM→Format→Answer
